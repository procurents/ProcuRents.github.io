The Problem
Your job is running the office.
Not chasing rental vendors.
📋

Quoting is a full-time job by itself

Getting quotes from multiple vendors, comparing rates, tracking availability — it takes hours. Then the job changes and you start over. Every request pulls you away from higher-value work.

📄

Purchase orders pile up fast

Every rental needs a PO. Multiple jobsites mean dozens of open orders at once — each one needing to be matched, tracked, approved, and filed. One missed PO creates a billing nightmare.

💥

Invoice disputes drain your time and energy

Invoices come in that don't match the quote, the contract, or what was even delivered. Disputing them means calls, emails, documentation — and vendors that aren't in a rush to fix it.

🔧

Damage and repair bills appear out of nowhere

Vendors charge for damage — sometimes legitimately, sometimes not. Without documentation and review, these charges just get paid. Reconciling them after the fact is nearly impossible.

💳

Payments and balance updates are a moving target

Keeping track of what's been paid, what's disputed, what's open, and what's overdue across multiple vendors and jobsites is a constant juggling act with no clean system behind it.

📦

Unauthorized substitutions nobody told you about

The vendor sends a different piece of equipment — same price or higher. The field doesn't catch it. The invoice doesn't flag it. You pay full price for something nobody ordered.
