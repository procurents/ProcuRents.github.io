The Problem

Rental issues you shouldn't
have to deal with

You're running a jobsite. Vendor headaches shouldn't be part of the job. Here's what ProcuRents takes off your plate.

🚧

Logistics coordination is a full-time headache

Sourcing equipment, confirming availability, scheduling delivery, coordinating with vendors who don't answer — all while trying to run a crew. Every minute on hold is a minute off the job.

📞

Off-rent calloffs nobody follows through on

The job wrapped, equipment's sitting idle, and it's still billing. Getting a vendor to process an off-rent and stop the clock can take days. Every one of those days costs your company money.

🧾

Invoice disputes that go nowhere

Wrong rates. Equipment that was never there. Fees not in the contract. You flag it, the vendor shrugs. You spend time on the phone instead of on the job — and the invoice gets paid wrong anyway.

🏢

Vendor account setup slows everything down

New vendor, new project — credit applications, references, contact chains. It takes time your schedule doesn't have. One delay on account setup can hold up an entire order.

💸

Pricing disputes with vendors in the field

The rate on the invoice doesn't match what was quoted. The vendor claims it was a different machine class. You're arguing about a contract you didn't write while your crew waits.

The Fix

One call to us.

Your crew contacts ProcuRents directly with what they need. We take it from there — so you stay focused on running the job, not chasing vendors.

01

02

When the equipment's done, contact us. We coordinate the pickup, confirm off-rent timing, and make sure billing stops when it should — not when the vendor gets around to it.

Wrong rate? Equipment not delivered? Surprise fees? We review every invoice against the contract and quote, dispute anything that doesn't match, and handle the back-and-forth directly.

If a vendor charges more than quoted, we catch it and resolve it. You don't get pulled into contract arguments. We handle it, document it, and move on.
