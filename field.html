The Problem
Rental issues you shouldn't
have to deal with
🚧

Logistics coordination is a full-time headache

Sourcing equipment, confirming availability, scheduling delivery, coordinating with vendors who don't answer — all while trying to run a crew. Every minute on hold with a rental company is a minute off the job.

📞

Off-rent calloffs nobody follows through on

The job wrapped, equipment's sitting idle, and it's still billing. Getting a vendor to actually process an off-rent — and stop the clock — can take days. And every one of those days costs your company money.

🧾

Invoice disputes that go nowhere

Wrong rates. Equipment that was never there. Fees that weren't in the contract. You flag it, the vendor shrugs, and you spend time on the phone instead of on the job. The invoice gets paid wrong anyway.

🏢

Vendor account setup slows everything down

New vendor, new project — and now you need to set up an account before you can even place an order. Credit applications, references, contact chains. It takes time your schedule doesn't have.

💸

Pricing disputes with vendors in the field

The rate on the invoice doesn't match what was quoted. The vendor claims it was a different machine class. You're arguing about a contract you didn't write while your crew waits. It shouldn't be your fight.

🔄

Wrong equipment shows up — and you deal with it

The vendor substitutes a different machine without telling anyone. It shows up, it doesn't work for the task, and now you're making calls to fix it while the crew stands around. That delay is on the project schedule.
