The Problem

What's actually eating your margin

Rental spend is one of the most unmanaged cost centers in construction. Here's where the profit goes.

01

Jobsite pricing is all over the map

Different superintendents call different vendors. No standardized rates. No negotiated contracts. Your company pays three different prices for the same equipment across three jobs — and nobody knows it.

02

Reduced jobsite efficiency from rental delays

Equipment that arrives late, wrong, or not at all brings crews to a halt. Every idle hour is a direct cost. Without proactive procurement, your teams pay the price in downtime.

03

Rental spend you can't fully see or control

Rentals placed in the field with no centralized oversight. Invoices flow through the office without systematic review. By the time someone notices an overcharge, it's already been paid.

04

Admin overhead eating into your team's productivity

Your PMs and office staff spend hours on quoting, PO management, invoice disputes, and vendor coordination. Billable thinking going to administrative overhead.

05

Equipment sitting on-rent after the job wraps

Off-renting gets missed. Equipment idles on a completed site and keeps billing. Days of unnecessary charges accumulate because nobody owns the off-rent process end-to-end.

06

You're renting what should be purchased — or vice versa

Without systematic review, ongoing rentals persist past the point where buying or leasing would be more cost-effective. The decision never gets made because no one's tracking it.
