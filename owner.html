01

Jobsite pricing is all over the map

Different superintendents call different vendors. No standardized rates. No negotiated contracts. Your company is paying three different prices for the same piece of equipment across three different jobs — and nobody knows it.

02

Reduced jobsite efficiency from rental delays

Equipment that arrives late, wrong, or not at all brings a crew to a halt. Every idle hour on a jobsite is a direct cost. When procurement isn't managed proactively, your crews pay the price in downtime.

03

Rental spend you can't fully see or control

Rentals get placed in the field with no centralized oversight. Invoices flow through the office without systematic review. By the time someone notices an overcharge, it's already been paid — or it's buried in a stack of others.

04

Admin overhead eating into your team's productivity

Your project managers and office staff are spending hours on quoting, PO management, invoice disputes, and vendor coordination. That's billable thinking going to administrative overhead that shouldn't require their attention.

05

Equipment sitting on-rent after the job wraps

Off-renting gets missed. Equipment sits idle on a completed site and keeps billing. Days of unnecessary charges accumulate because nobody owns the off-rent process end-to-end.

06

You're renting what should be purchased — or vice versa

Without a systematic review, ongoing rentals persist well past the point where buying or leasing would have been more cost-effective. The decision never gets made because no one's tracking it.
