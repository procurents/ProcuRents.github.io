How ProcuRents Works

Your employees contact us directly from the field. We handle everything else — with RentFWD running at the checkpoints that protect your spend.

1

Your Team Requests from the Field

Your crew or PM reaches ProcuRents directly — by phone or text from the job site. No vendor relationships to manage, no portals to log into.

2

We Shop & Negotiate

We compare vendors, negotiate rates, and select the best value for your project — including your preferred accounts and national deals, while respecting vendors you’ve asked us never to use.

3

We Place & Track the Order

ProcuRents handles POs, verifies contract terms match the quote, and coordinates delivery. Designated approvers can sign off from their phone when your company requires it.

4

We Manage Invoices

Every invoice reviewed against what was quoted and agreed — errors, duplicate charges, and discrepancies caught before payment.

5

We Handle Off-Renting

We coordinate pickup, verify final billing, and close the rental clean. Site moves and partial call-offs handled while the job is still running — no stale rentals, no surprise charges.

Services

What We Manage For You

Every administrative function of the rental lifecycle — completely off your plate.

Vendor Shopping & Sourcing

We compare rental vendors on your behalf so you get competitive pricing and equipment availability — with your preferred accounts prioritized and blocked vendors never contacted.

Order Placement & Tracking

We place every order, assign PO numbers, and keep your records clean, organized, and audit-ready. Approvals handled on your terms when your company requires sign-off.

Compliance & Account Setup

Insurance certificates, tax-exempt documentation, and vendor credit setup — handled so rentals don’t stall waiting on paperwork.

Invoice Management

We review, reconcile, and dispute rental invoices — catching errors, duplicate charges, and discrepancies before they hit your books.

Off-Renting & Closeout

When the job wraps, we coordinate pickup, verify final billing, and close the rental cleanly. Mid-job site moves and partial call-offs included.
